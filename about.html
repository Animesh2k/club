Our Sangha is a family. Through annual gatherings, cultural programs, and social events, we build strong, lasting bonds among all our members and visitors.

Service

Beyond festivities, we are dedicated to social welfare. From blood donation camps to free emergency ambulances, service to humanity is our core principle.

80 Years of Devotion

1986

Journey Begins

Our historic journey begins. With great devotion, we organize our first universal Durga Puja festival.

1986

1992

Anna Seva Milestone

This year is a milestone in our Anna Seva. Around 4,400 people participated in the Anna Seva for Mother's Bhog.

1992

1995

Official Permission

We, along with a few clubs in the undivided Midnapore district, received permission from the Midnapore district administration to organize Durga Puja.

1995

1996

Concrete Building

This year the club's own concrete building was built.

1996

1999

Official Registration

We are registered as a social organization under the Society Act, Government of West Bengal.

1999

2004

Guide Map Inclusion

For the first time, our Durga Puja has been included in the Puja Guide Map published by Purba Medinipur District Police.

2004

2009

Notable Members

The then MP from Tamluk, currently the Honorable Chief Minister of West Bengal, Shri Suvendu Adhikari, accepted the life membership of our organization.

2009

2012

Ambulance Service Starts

Our ambulance service was started on the recommendation of the honorable MP of Tamluk and a life member of our organization, Shri Suvendu Adhikari, with the financial support of MCPI Haldia (CRS Fund).

2012

2016

Parir Desh Theme

Our first themed pandal made of cotton - 'Parir Desh'. The biggest crowd ever gathered at our pandal. The puja was inaugurated by the Honorable District Magistrate and Superintendent of Police. Purba Medinipur.

2016

2020

Covid-19 Response

A new challenge was before us, we jumped into human service. We distributed adequate quantities of masks and sanitizers. We even distributed some PPE kits.

2020
